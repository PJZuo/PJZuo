About Me

Peijie Zuo is a Lecturer at Institute of NBC Defence, PLA Army. His research mainly focuses on environmental analytical chemistry, persistent organic pollutants analysis, atmospheric particulate matter source apportionment and related environmental risk assessment.

Research Interests

  • Atmospheric particulate‑bound contaminants
  • Source apportionment of atmospheric particulate matter
